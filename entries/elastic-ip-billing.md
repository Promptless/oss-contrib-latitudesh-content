Elastic IPs are now a billable resource. Billing starts when you provision an Elastic IP and stops when you release it.

**Billing intervals:**

- **On-demand projects**: Monthly billing
- **Reserved projects**: Yearly billing

Elastic IP charges appear as a line item on your project invoice.

[Learn more about Elastic IPs](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/networking/elastic-ips)
