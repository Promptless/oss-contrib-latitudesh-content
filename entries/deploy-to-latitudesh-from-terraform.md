We are excited to announce that you can now deploy and version your Latitude.sh infrastructure with Terraform.

If you are an experienced Terraform user or just getting started, we've put together a [guide](https://docs.latitude.sh/docs/integrating-terraform-with-latitudesh) that can get you up and running with Latitude.sh's provider in no time.

Thanks to the [Capture Alpha](https://capturealpha.com/) team for their valuable contribution to this project. 🤝

[Check out the docs](https://docs.latitude.sh/docs/terraform) or go to the [provider page](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/latitudesh/latitudesh/latest/docs).
