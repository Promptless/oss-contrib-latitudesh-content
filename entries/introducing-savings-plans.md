Savings Plans are now available for on-demand compute resources.

Users can now maximize savings with discounts for long-term monthly spend commitments:

- 12-month term:** 30% discount**
- 36-month term: **50% discount**

<u>Here's how it works</u>

You can now commit to a specific monthly expenditure for compute resources, **prepay for the desired duration,** and receive discounts based on the commitment length.

Each month, the committed amount offsets the on-demand compute usage on your project bill.

Head to [Settings> Savings Plans](https://www.latitude.sh/dashboard/settings/savings-plans) to create your first plan, or check out our Docs to [learn more](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/billing/savings-plans).
