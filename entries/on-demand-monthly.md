We announced [hourly billing](https://www.latitude.sh/changelog/hourly-billing) earlier this year to remove the friction of getting started with Latitude.sh. It is great when you want to run PoCs or to quickly scale up and down. For predictable workloads, it is often better to have consistent, linear pricing for your resources.

Today, we are adding the ability for you to choose between hourly and monthly billing when deploying a new server. With monthly billing you** save 5% compared to the hourly rate**.

The same project can have both hourly and monthly servers. You choose how to be billed at the server level, giving you a lot of flexibility when building your infrastructure.

[Check out the docs →](https://docs.latitude.sh/docs/on-demand-vs-reserved)
