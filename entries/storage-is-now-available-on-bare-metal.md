Our platform now allows you to mount a filesystem on your bare metal servers for persistent storage, keeping your data across reboots and reinstalls. This means you can: 

- Expand small instances with high-performance storage without upgrading to larger ones.
- Scale your storage as data grows while maintaining performance.

[Check out the Docs to learn more](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/storage/filesystem)
