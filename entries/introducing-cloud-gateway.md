We are excited to introduce [Cloud Gateway](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/networking/cloud-gateway), a straightforward solution for connecting your Latitude.sh servers to major cloud providers: 

- **Cut costs and gain control: **Lower your compute and bandwidth costs while taking full control of your servers.
- **Fast, reliable networking:** Best-in-class network connectivity and seamless cloud integration supported by [Megaport’s](https://www.megaport.com/) global infrastructure.
- **Customizable performance: **Match your bandwidth needs with adjustable port speeds.
