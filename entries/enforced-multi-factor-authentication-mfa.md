We have added extra security layers to protect your account and streamline your login experience. Here's what’s new:

- **MFA enforcement: **Account owners can now enforce [Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/settings/mfa) for all members. Simply go to *Settings & Billing > Members* and enable "Enforce MFA". Once enabled, every team member will have to set up MFA to access the team.
- **SAML SSO-exclusive login:** Users with [SAML SSO](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/settings/sso) can no longer log in using other authentication methods, such as [OAuth](https://oauth.net/2/) and passwords. This ensures that SAML SSO remains their sole login method.
