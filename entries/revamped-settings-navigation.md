We’ve improved how you navigate key settings in the [dashboard](https://www.latitude.sh/dashboard). Here’s what’s changed:

- **Unified management:** Project and Team settings are now available in one centralized menu, so you can manage everything without switching pages.
- **Usage & billing:** We’ve redesigned the Usage and Billing sections, making it easier to navigate between your usage overview, invoices, and billing info.
- **New settings sidebar: **The new collapsible sidebar offers quicker access to your settings.
