We’re excited to introduce the beta version of [GPU virtual machines](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/vms/gpu-instances). Powered by NVIDIA’s L40S and H100, they are a more straightforward alternative for workloads leveraging single GPUs.


Here’s what is included with each instance:

- **Up to 80GB** of VRAM
- **500 GB** of local NVMe
- **Dua**l **10 Gbps** networking for high-speed data transfer.
- **$ 0.81 / GPU / hr**  on-demand


All GPU instances are deployed in Dallas, US, with Ubuntu 24.04.
