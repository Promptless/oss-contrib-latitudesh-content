Say hello to [Capacity Reservations](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/servers/capacity-reservation), a feature designed to ensure you get the servers you need as soon as they become available.

With Capacity Reservations you can:

- View and reserve servers before they become available
- See which servers will be added to our fleet and when
- Track the delivery status directly from your [project’s page](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/projects)

[<u>Learn more</u>](https://www.latitude.sh/docs/servers/capacity-reservation)
