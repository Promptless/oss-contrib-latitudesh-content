We’ve expanded our network options for crypto payments. The Ethereum network has been added, and you can now purchase credits using tokens through either the Solana or Ethereum networks.

When adding credits, select your preferred network, choose your desired token, and sync the respective wallet.

Visit the [<u>Billing</u>](https://www.latitude.sh/dashboard/billing) page to get started.
